Our paper [G²IFWI: Gradient-coupled diffusion priors for regularizing implicit full waveform inversion](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026JH001488), led by Linrong Wang, is published in *JGR: Machine Learning and Computation*: the score gradient of a diffusion-model prior is optimized jointly with the physics-based gradient of implicit FWI, and well logs can be added through guidance when they are available.
