Our paper [Seismic data interpolation using implicit neural representations with Fourier-hash features](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aiig.2026.100287), led by Linrong Wang, is published open access in *Artificial Intelligence in Geosciences*: Fourier features carry the smooth, continuous structure early in training and multi-resolution hash features take over the local detail later, all learned self-supervised from the recorded traces.
